Your love
chart.

A value–time curve like the one on the book’s cover. Split your relationship into six periods and mark the value you got from it in each period, from 1 to 10. Drag the points, or select one and adjust it with the arrow keys. Then get your market commentary.

What am I drawing? Value is what the relationship added to your life in that period: happiness, trust, peace, excitement. There is no single right answer. Mark what you felt.

How much has the love industry sold you?

Your friend's result

Ritual Customer

Making love visible matters to you: the right day, the right gesture, the right gift. Rituals can truly add meaning to a relationship. But the book separates a shared ritual from a compulsory performance. Once the thought “If we don’t do this, people will think our relationship is failing” kicks in, the same gesture can turn into pressure from social judgement. The market supplies the tools of a ritual; the meaning the couple gives it decides whose ritual it is. This index is a thinking tool, not a verdict.

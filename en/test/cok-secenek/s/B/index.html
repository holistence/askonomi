Are too many options making you unhappy?

Your friend's result

The imaginary rival

Even after choosing, you keep comparing: one person's humour, another's romance, someone else's success. Comparison can be useful, a good habit you see in another couple can improve your own relationship. But the book points to a trap: if you build a composite partner in your head from everyone's strongest traits, a real person can never win. Abundance creates the toughest rival of all: the perfect alternative who never existed.

The “Seen” economy: what signal do your messages send?

Your friend's result

The Time Signal

You measure interest not by the length of a message, but by the time someone gives you. According to the book, time can be a strong signal because it can’t be taken back: money can be earned again, but a passing hour can’t be brought back. Time given by a very busy person can carry a high opportunity cost. Your risk: reading the size of a feeling from the amount of time. Without knowing the person’s alternatives and life circumstances, this calculation can easily go wrong.
